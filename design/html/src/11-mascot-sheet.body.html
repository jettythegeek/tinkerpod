One robot, built from the logo's pod head: navy screen-face, orange antenna, yellow ear-pods and the cog, code and wrench toolkit. Its mood follows the child's progress, so the face is the first thing a kid reads.

Motion cues (for animation)

• Cheering: jumps, stars burst, antenna spins
• Thinking: eyes look up, “?” pulses
• Sleepy: antenna droops, “z” floats, gets brighter when a lesson starts
